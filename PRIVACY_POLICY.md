# Calcverse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 August 2026_

Calcverse ("the app") is designed to work entirely offline. All calculations run on your device
and are never transmitted to us.

## 1. Data we store

Calculation history, favorites and preferences are stored **only** in a local database on your
device. You can clear this data at any time from the History and Settings screens. Uninstalling the
app removes it.

## 2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personally identifiable information. We do not
sell data to third parties.

## 3. Advertising

The app may display ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve
relevant ads. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details. You
may reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

## 4. Analytics & crash reporting

If enabled in a release build, anonymous, aggregated usage and crash data may be collected (via
Firebase) to improve stability. **No calculation contents are ever included.** In builds where this
is disabled, no analytics or crash data leaves your device.

## 5. Permissions

- **Microphone** — used only for optional voice input, processed by your device's speech
  recognizer. Audio is not stored or uploaded by Calcverse.
- **Internet / Network state** — used only for advertising and the in-app review prompt. The
  calculators themselves never require a network connection.

## 6. Children

Calcverse does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and
in the in-app copy (Settings → Privacy policy).

## 8. Contact

Questions? Reach us through the app's listing on Google Play.

By using Calcverse you agree to this policy.
